# Restaurant bill splitter

This site provides an array of tasty items each with a unique price.

Check off boxes for your items, tally your party number with a dropdown, use the tip slider to show your appreciation and the total is calculated along with the bill split into portions based on party size(tax included based on CA LA tax rate!

The formula used: Total = subtotal + tax(subtotal * taxrate) + tip(subtotal * (tippercent/100))
  per person = total / numPeople
